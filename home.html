What this is

The best data
in sports, in one place.

Onyx Sports Data is where you do the research before you build a slip. We pull the best public data across the industry, from pitch tracking to box scores to injury reports, run it through models built from scratch for each sport, and lay it out game by game.

Every projection sits next to the live Onyx price, so you see exactly where the model and the market disagree. Every pick is graded in public, wins and losses alike, so you can judge the model before you trust it.
