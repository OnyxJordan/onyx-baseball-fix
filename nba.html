How the model works

1. Minutes first

Every projection starts with how long a player will be on the floor. Last season's minutes per game act as a prior, and this season's games (including nights he did not play) pull it toward his current role, with extra weight on the last three games. Each team's minutes are then fitted to the 240 available: when a teammate is OUT or Doubtful, his minutes flow to the rest of the rotation, and deep benches are trimmed first when a roster over-counts. Back-to-backs and lopsided spreads shave heavy-minute players.

2. Production per minute

Points, rebounds and assists per minute blend last season (as a 300-minute prior) with what he is doing now, so a hot week moves the number but does not hijack it. Players with no NBA history start from a positional league rate.

3. The game around him

Each team's player points are fitted to the team total implied by the Onyx spread and total. Rebounds and assists scale with game pace and with what the opponent allows, regressed by how many games it has played.

4. Priced against Onyx

Projections become over and under probabilities with a right-skewed distribution (a typical night lands a little under a player's average, a big night pulls the average up). Those are blended with the vig-free Onyx market, which carries most of the weight because it is the sharper single read, and edge is measured against the price you actually get. A pick needs real edge, a sane price, recent form on its side and a player with no injury tag, and it is locked the moment it qualifies. Every pick is graded against the final box score and stays on the Record tab, wins and losses alike.

5. Backtested before it shipped

The model was replayed over the full 2025-26 season one night at a time, seeing only what was known before tip: 26,352 player-games and 17,668 real Onyx prop lines. Settings were tuned on October through January and judged only on February through April. On those unseen games it beat every simple baseline (points miss 5.15 vs 5.48 for the season average), its probabilities matched reality (it claimed 54.2% on its picks and they hit 54.0%), and the picks lane went 74-63 at Onyx opening prices. That is a small sample, so the live Record tab is the scoreboard that counts.

Projections are estimates, not guarantees. Bet responsibly.
